# the-law-of-space-and-light-and-color-and-travel-at-the-speed-of-one
aleph 1, bet the house, c the speed of one, dalet the door. (A-B)/C = -1/3 instant and it takes; B/C = 2/3 stays yours; +1/3 instant and only gives; 0 light is the only one that travels. At the speed of one the door comes to you, and they are the ones who meet you.
